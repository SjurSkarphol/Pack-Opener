# AI Pack Opener
VibeCode Pack Opener

**Pack Opener** is a fantasy trading-card game you play in the browser. You start with 500 gold and spend 100 to open a pack of five cards. Each card is one of five rarities, from Common to Legendary, and glows in its rarity colour when you hover over it before flipping. Every card you find goes into your inventory, which works like a collection book: owned cards in colour, missing ones greyed out. You can sell cards for gold to open more packs, aiming to collect all 18.
